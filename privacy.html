Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Hawleywood LLC ("we," "us," "our") develops mobile applications, including Coral Companion. This policy explains what information our apps collect and how it's used.

Information We Collect

Our apps are designed to work offline and store your data (progress, notes, preferences) locally on your device. We do not require account creation and do not collect personal information unless you voluntarily provide it, such as an email address submitted through a "Notify Me" form.

Analytics & Third Parties

If a specific app uses analytics or crash reporting services, this will be disclosed in that app's store listing and, where applicable, in-app settings.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share data with third parties except as required to operate the app (e.g., app store platforms) or comply with the law.

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of any information you've voluntarily submitted to us by contacting us below.
